THE THINKING / 01

Make the online reading experience feel like slowing down.

Fieldnotes is an illustrative concept for a digital nature journal. The design explores how an editorial website can create a sense of calm without getting in the reader’s way: generous spacing, a clear reading hierarchy, and color inspired by the landscapes in its stories.

The approach

I started with the reading experience and worked outward. The type pairing gives headlines a little character while keeping body text comfortable at every screen size. The layout shifts with the viewport, so the journal feels intentional on a phone as well as a large display.

What this concept explores

  • A flexible editorial layout that gives photography and writing room to breathe.
  • Readable type sizes, strong contrast, and a clear keyboard-friendly structure.
  • A reusable set of page patterns for journals, story pages, and issue collections.
Concept project: Fieldnotes is a fictional portfolio exercise. Its design, content, and outcomes are illustrative, not client work.
