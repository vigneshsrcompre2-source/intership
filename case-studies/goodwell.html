THE THINKING / 02

Make starting feel as easy as taking a breath.

Goodwell is a fictional wellness service concept. The challenge was to imagine an online experience that feels reassuring rather than demanding. A soft palette, a direct invitation, and short sessions all help keep the interface approachable.

The approach

Instead of filling the first screen with features, the design highlights a single clear next step. Interactive elements are kept familiar, text is concise, and the visual rhythm leaves room to pause. The responsive layout keeps the central action easy to find on a smaller screen.

What this concept explores

  • Clear visual hierarchy for a focused, low-friction start.
  • Responsive components with visible focus states and accessible controls.
  • Gentle interactions that support the content rather than compete with it.
Concept project: Goodwell is a fictional portfolio exercise. Its design, content, and outcomes are illustrative, not client work.
