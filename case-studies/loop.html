THE THINKING / 03

Make progress visible, without making perfection the goal.

Loop is a fictional habit tracker concept built around a simple idea: missing one day should not erase the effort that came before it. The weekly view focuses on consistency and a friendly next step instead of streaks, warnings, or guilt.

The approach

The dashboard turns the week into a small, scannable snapshot. Color distinguishes completed days while keeping the remaining days neutral and inviting. The interface is designed to adapt gracefully to narrow screens, with readable labels and generous tap targets.

What this concept explores

  • A calm progress visualization that makes state clear without relying on color alone.
  • Simple, reusable interface patterns for habits, weekly views, and encouraging feedback.
  • A lightweight frontend approach that keeps the main interaction quick to understand.
Concept project: Loop is a fictional portfolio exercise. Its design, content, and outcomes are illustrative, not client work.
